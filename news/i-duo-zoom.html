Display Zoom can’t be changed after Duo setup

The Default/Medium/Large text size is chosen during setup, but the usual Settings > Appearance > Display Zoom option is missing afterwards on iOS 27.2. One owner says Apple Support told them the only fix is to erase and set up again. Apple hasn’t commented.
