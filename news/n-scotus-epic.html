Supreme Court will hear Apple’s Epic appeal on 11 January

Oral arguments are set for 11 January 2027. Apple is appealing the 2025 contempt ruling that said it broke the original injunction by limiting external payment links and charging commission on outside purchases. The court is reviewing only the contempt finding; what Apple may charge is being handled separately by the district court.
