Dating apps must carry an 18+ rating on the App Store

From 16 November, developers must say whether their apps include dating or relationship features, including virtual companions with romantic content, and whether they share real-world location. Dating apps will need a minimum 18+ rating (A18 in Brazil). Everyone has until 12 January 2027 to submit both answers in App Store Connect.
