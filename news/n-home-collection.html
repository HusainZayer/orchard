Apple Home Collection: cameras, a lock and thermostats

Alongside HomeView, Apple introduced a line of smart home accessories made with LG and Schneider Electric: a video doorbell, three cameras, a deadbolt lock that unlocks as you approach, two thermostats and room sensors, from $39.95. They set up with one tap from iPhone and arrive in December, with some in early 2027.
