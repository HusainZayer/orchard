HomeView details: a mirror, guest controls and how it checks who you are

Apple's new HomeView support pages and early hands-on reports fill in some details. A Mirror option in Control Center shows the camera view with an optional edge light and 1x or 2x zoom. Owners can limit what unrecognized guests can do with Safari, Siri and explicit content. To show personal content, HomeView checks your face and that your iPhone or iPad is nearby, recently unlocked and on the same Wi-Fi; after repeated failed tries, face recognition is switched off until you confirm with a registered device. Apple says it may not recognize a covered face or one in sunglasses. There is no power button: restart it in Settings or unplug it briefly. Notifications are limited to calls and home devices, with an option for critical alerts.
