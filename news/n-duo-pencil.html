iPhone Duo gets Apple Pencil support

iOS 27.2 brings Apple Pencil to iPhone Duo’s inner display, earlier than Apple’s “later this year” promise. Only the USB-C Apple Pencil works; Apple Pencil Pro and older models aren’t supported.
