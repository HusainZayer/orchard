Apple posts a record September quarter: $114.5 billion

Revenue rose 12% to $114.5 billion and earnings per share were $2.10, up 14%. Apple reports record iPhone revenue for the quarter, an all-time high for Services and a record $479 billion for the fiscal year. Tariff refunds added about $0.05 to earnings per share.
