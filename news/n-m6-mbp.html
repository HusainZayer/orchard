The 14-inch MacBook Pro moves to M6

Apple announced the 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6 by press release: a 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU, a dual 16-core Neural Engine, up to 170GB/s memory bandwidth, and Wi‑Fi 7 with Bluetooth 6. Apple says graphics are up to 30% faster than M5. It starts at $1,999 with 1TB, in Space Black and Silver. Pre-orders open October 28; it arrives November 6. The design is unchanged.
