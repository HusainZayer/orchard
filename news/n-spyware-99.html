Apple sends spyware warnings to users in 99 countries

Apple sent a new round of threat notifications saying some people may have been targeted by mercenary spyware. The Financial Times, as relayed by 9to5Mac, says German officials were among those warned and that the attacks reportedly used a zero-click flaw. Apple does not name who is behind it and recommends Lockdown Mode for recipients.
