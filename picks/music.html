  1. CD Wally Rank 10 of 100

    Flip through album discs in a virtual CD wallet. Opened like a book, the wallet spine sits on the fold with the disc on one side and tracks on the other; half-folded, the disc spins on top with controls below.

    • Built around the fold
    • 2 unfolded postures: Open, Laptop
    • Confirmed in release notes (v3.5.1)
  2. Spinstack Rank 23 of 100

    Vinyl collection app tracking record condition. Pages split across the fold in book or tabletop posture; open, Ambient Mode spins a record on a turntable and the gatefold cover swings on its hinge.

    • Built around the fold
    • 2 unfolded postures: Open, Laptop
    • Confirmed in release notes (v1.6)
  3. Bellowsby Rank 35 of 100

    A virtual accordion you play by squeezing the phone, with the hinge as the bellows.

    • Built around the fold
    • Works open on the inner display
    • Confirmed in release notes (v1.1)
  4. DuoBeat Rank 43 of 100

    Pocket drum machine, sampler, and step sequencer for one-handed play. Half-folded, it becomes an instrument with the screen above the fold and pads below; closed, the groovebox fits the cover display; folding mid-beat triggers a riser that drops on the next bar.

    • Built around the fold
    • 2 unfolded postures: Open, Laptop
  5. Fold Accordion Rank 46 of 100

    The hinge is the bellows: opening and closing the phone plays notes, and a learn mode highlights the keys to press.

    • Built around the fold
    • Works open on the inner display
    • Confirmed in release notes (v1.0.1)
  6. Isai Rank 50 of 100

    Hi-res music player for files from cloud and local storage. Pure Music mode turns it into a retro cassette, CD or turntable player: the player on the cover display when closed, records beside the machine when open.

    • Built around the fold
    • 2 unfolded postures: Open, Split View
  7. My Vinyl+ Rank 58 of 100

    Organizes vinyl, CDs, and cassettes, synced with Discogs. Open, a record sits on one side beside its details, and opening the hinge slides the record out of its sleeve.

    • Built around the fold
    • 2 unfolded postures: Open, Split View
  8. Tutti Rank 74 of 100

    Paperless music stand for sheet music. Held like a book, two score pages sit side by side; on a table, controls move to the bottom so the music stays clear.

    • 3 unfolded postures: Open, Split View, Laptop
    • Confirmed in release notes (v3.4)
  9. Twirlaby Rank 76 of 100

    A jewellery music box that lifts its lid as you unfold the phone.

    • Built around the fold
    • Works open on the inner display
    • Confirmed in release notes (v1.1)
